Start investigating with confidence.

A beginner-friendly guide to the ChaosLab workflow, from starting a mission to reporting the evidence.

01

Create an account

Register, verify your email and log in. Your account gives you access to the dashboard, plans and saved history.

02

Start a mission

Open Singleplayer, choose Easy, Medium or Hard, and optionally use a seed when you want to reproduce the same scenario.

03

Read the evidence

Use the virtual filesystem to inspect the mission brief, access logs and supporting files. Look for repeated source IPs, unusual requests, authentication failures and suspicious status codes.

04

Submit your findings

Report each attacker's source IP and the actions associated with that attacker. The server checks your findings and returns a score.

05

Review and improve

Check your score and History page. After submission, rate the mission and send comments to the admin to help improve future scenarios.

TIP

Start by reading the mission file. It tells you how many objectives to find, which keeps your investigation focused.

Multiplayer and classrooms

Multiplayer lets you create or join a room and compare completion times. Institution users can join classrooms created by faculty, complete assigned games and view classroom results.
